How to Play

  1. Enter your P&L. Tap + WIN or - LOSS, then type the amount.
  2. Pick a template. Check the Featured carousel or scroll the full gallery and tap any pixel-art card.
  3. Add banners (optional). Tap one or more banners — numbers show the stack order, tap again to remove.
  4. Nudge the P&L. Use the vertical slider on the left of the preview to move it up or down, the X slider below to slide it left or right, and the SIZE slider to make it bigger or smaller. Hit RESET to snap back to the template default.
  5. Pick a color. Tap a COLOR swatch to recolor the P&L number. The A swatch keeps the template's tuned color.
  6. Preview updates live on the canvas.
  7. Download PNG and post it to your trading chat.

Tip: nothing leaves your device — no signup, no upload. It's all rendered right in your browser.
